Month currency rule: a month's currency = that month's limit segment normalCurrency (segments before a D-07 change keep the old currency; current+future segments are converted by plan 15); no segment → the category's current currency. Closed month (< openMonth) with month currency ≠ current currency → D-17 conversion path. Every other month → native path (each tx converted to the current currency at its date).
Effective limit in a converted month = cushion limit if the budget was in CUSHION mode that month (cushion history), else normal; if cushionCurrency ≠ normalCurrency, convert the cushion limit into the month currency at the closing rate first.
Close rate = rate(monthCcy → currentCcy) as of the month's last day. Missing rate (NoFxRateAvailable) → treat the tx/month as unconvertible: skip it and set `fxUnavailable: true` on the loader result (never fail the replay).
