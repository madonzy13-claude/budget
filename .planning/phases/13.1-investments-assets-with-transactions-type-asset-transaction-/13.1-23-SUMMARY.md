# 13.1-23 — Sell-into destination on the quantity × price model (UAT 9, 10, 14)

**One quantity + one price. Price: Existing = asset's own (read-only); New = typed, else the instrument's live price (HoldingFormFields now emits `livePriceCents` and no longer renders "Quantity bought"). Price NEVER follows quantity (user, UAT 10). Quantity follows sold value ÷ price only while untouched (`qtyTouched`); a hand-typed quantity is never overwritten, even when quantity × price ≠ sold value. Received = quantity × price → quantity destinations have no Amount received and omit `receivedCents` (13.1-18 contract). Realized preview = received ÷ rate(source → received) − sold cost, in the source currency — 0 for a fair PLN→EUR swap.**
