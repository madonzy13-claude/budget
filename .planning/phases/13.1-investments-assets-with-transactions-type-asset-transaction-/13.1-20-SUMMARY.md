# 13.1-20 — Ledger strip behaves like every swipe row (UAT 4, 5, 12, 13, 14)

**useSwipeReveal holds the asset row's gesture verbatim (plus an onOffset(px, live) callback) and drives both asset rows and ledger rows. The open ledger follows its asset's swipe (no transition mid-gesture), slides open/closed via grid-rows 0fr↔1fr (reduced-motion off), and closes the card with rounded bottom corners. Ledger delete: red when deletable, grey aria-disabled + popover reason otherwise; tap no longer reveals; desktop (sm+) shows the button at the row end. Dates carry the year; no "auto calculated"; convert legs show stored amount/currency; Swap is var(--info) blue; confirm action is Button variant destructive.**
